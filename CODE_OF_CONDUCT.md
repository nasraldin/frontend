# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At Ajrly, we are committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative
environment where everyone can contribute to our mission of building innovative
technology solutions. We believe that diversity of thought, experience, and
background leads to better outcomes for our community and our users.

## Our Values

We are guided by these core values:

- **Respect:** We treat everyone with dignity and respect, valuing diverse
  perspectives
- **Inclusion:** We actively work to create an environment where all voices are
  heard
- **Collaboration:** We believe in the power of working together to achieve great
  things
- **Excellence:** We strive for high-quality contributions and continuous
  improvement
- **Integrity:** We act with honesty, transparency, and accountability

## Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to:

### Positive Contributions

- **Use inclusive language** that welcomes and respects all individuals
- **Be respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Give and receive constructive feedback** gracefully and professionally
- **Focus on what is best for the community** and our users
- **Show empathy** towards other community members
- **Help newcomers** feel welcome and supported
- **Celebrate contributions** and acknowledge the work of others
- **Ask questions** when you need clarification
- **Be patient** with others who may be learning or have different skill levels

### Professional Standards

- **Be constructive** in your feedback and suggestions
- **Stay on topic** in discussions and avoid derailing conversations
- **Respect time zones** and availability of other contributors
- **Use appropriate channels** for different types of communication
- **Follow project guidelines** and established processes
- **Maintain confidentiality** when required

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

### Harassment and Discrimination

- **Harassment** in any form, including verbal, written, or physical
- **Discrimination** based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
  and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
  religion, sexual identity and orientation, or any other personal characteristics
- **Sexual harassment** including unwelcome sexual attention, advances, or comments
- **Bullying** or intimidation of any kind
- **Stalking** or following community members

### Inappropriate Communication

- **Trolling** or deliberately provocative behavior
- **Insulting or derogatory comments** about individuals or groups
- **Personal or political attacks** that are not relevant to the project
- **Offensive language** including slurs, epithets, or discriminatory language
- **Spam** or unsolicited commercial content
- **Impersonation** of other individuals or organizations

### Privacy and Security

- **Publishing private information** without explicit permission
- **Sharing confidential information** about the project or community members
- **Attempting to gain unauthorized access** to systems or data
- **Creating fake accounts** or misrepresenting your identity

### Other Unacceptable Behaviors

- **Threatening violence** or encouraging harm to others
- **Posting inappropriate content** including violent, sexual, or offensive material
- **Disrupting community events** or discussions
- **Refusing to follow** reasonable requests from project maintainers
- **Retaliating** against individuals who report violations

## Reporting Violations

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it immediately:

- **Email:** conduct@ajrly.com
- **GitHub Issues:** Create a private issue with the label `conduct`
- **Direct Contact:** Reach out to project maintainers directly

### What to Include in Your Report

To help us address the issue effectively, please include:

1. **Your contact information** (so we can follow up)
2. **Names of individuals involved** (including witnesses)
3. **Description of the incident** with as much detail as possible
4. **When and where** the incident occurred
5. **Any relevant context** or background information
6. **How you would like** the situation to be resolved

### Confidentiality

- All reports will be treated **confidentially**
- We will **not share your personal information** without your explicit permission
- We will **protect your privacy** throughout the investigation process
- You may report **anonymously**, though this may limit our ability to follow up

## Enforcement Process

### Investigation

1. **Acknowledgment:** We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 24 hours
2. **Investigation:** Our team will investigate the incident thoroughly and fairly
3. **Gathering Information:** We may need to speak with other individuals involved
4. **Decision:** We will determine appropriate action based on our findings
5. **Communication:** We will communicate the outcome to all parties involved

### Possible Actions

Depending on the severity and circumstances, we may take one or more of the
following actions:

- **Warning:** Private or public warning to the individual
- **Temporary Ban:** Temporary suspension from community participation
- **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from the community
- **Content Removal:** Removal of inappropriate content or contributions
- **Escalation:** Reporting to relevant authorities if necessary

### Appeals Process

If you disagree with a decision made by our team:

1. **Submit an appeal** in writing within 30 days
2. **Provide additional context** or information
3. **Request review** by a different team member
4. **Receive final decision** within 14 days

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with the Ajrly project,
including but not limited to:

- **GitHub repositories** and discussions
- **Official communication channels** (email, Slack, Discord)
- **Social media** when representing the project
- **Conferences and events** where project members are present
- **Online and offline interactions** related to the project

## Project Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- **Setting the tone** for community interactions
- **Leading by example** in following the Code of Conduct
- **Addressing violations** promptly and fairly
- **Maintaining confidentiality** of reports
- **Regular review** and updates of this policy
- **Training** new maintainers on enforcement procedures
